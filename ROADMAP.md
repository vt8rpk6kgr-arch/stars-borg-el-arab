# ROADMAP (v2+ Out of Scope Features)

As specified in Section 8.6 of the Master Prompt, the following items are explicitly planned for future phases:

- Online payment gateway integration (Vodafone Cash, Fawry, credit cards).
- SMS / OTP authentication verification.
- Web Push notifications for match countdowns and challenges.
- Native mobile wrapper (Capacitor/PWA enhancements).
- Live real-time in-game scoring with referee timer.
- Match video highlights and replay clips.
- In-app team chat room.
- Multi-pitch expansion (adding Pitch 2 and Pitch 3).
- Referee and pitch-marshal scheduling.
- Fan prediction mini-games with weekly leaderboards.
- Automated AI comic match reports generated after games.
- Full season historical archive.
